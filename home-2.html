Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our farm-to-table testing and delivery protocols.

Every carton and cotton mesh bag carries a unique cryptographic batch stamp (e.g. #FL-2026-889). Pointing any smartphone camera at this QR code immediately pulls up the harvest hour, exact soil microbiome test results, farmer profile, and multi-spectral 214-parameter pesticide residue test certificate.

Every harvest batch undergoes Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS) testing to verify 0.00 ppm for synthetic pesticides, heavy metals, and chemical ripening hormones. Produce is also checked for Brix sugar density and cellular moisture retention.

Harvest starts at 5:00 AM across our partnered family farms. Batches arrive at our regional temperature-controlled aggregation facility by 7:30 AM for rapid testing and packing in breathable cotton bags. Electric cold-chain vans dispatch orders directly to your doorstep between 8:30 AM and 11:00 AM.

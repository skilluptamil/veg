How to Keep Your Tomatoes Fresh

Do not refrigerate unripe tomatoes immediately, as cold temperatures below 12°C break down the aromatic flavor enzymes.

Room Temperature (Best Aroma)

Store stem-down in a cool, ventilated fruit basket away from direct sunlight for up to 5 days.

Extended Refrigeration

Once fully ripe, store in the crisper drawer for up to 10 days. Bring to room temp before cooking.

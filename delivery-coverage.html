Common Questions

Delivery & Logistics FAQs

Everything you need to know about our doorstep farm service.

All our electric delivery vans are equipped with custom temperature-regulated cargo compartments maintaining 12°C. Produce is packed in breathable, moisture-wicking pouches to prevent condensation and bruising.

To receive your vegetables at 6:00 AM freshly picked for breakfast preparation, please place your order before 9:30 PM the previous evening.

We have a 100% "No-Questions-Asked Freshness Guarantee." Simply click a photo in our app or WhatsApp our hotline within 12 hours for an instant replacement or full credit refund.

Our honeycomb corrugated cartons are 100% reusable and plastic-free. Whenever you accumulate 5 empty boxes, hand them to the delivery partner on your next drop. The driver scans your return QR and ₹50 is instantly credited to your FreshLeaf wallet.
